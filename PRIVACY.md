# Privacy Policy for CompassPins

**Last Updated:** October 27, 2024

## Information We Collect

CompassPins collects and uses the following information:

- **Google Account Information**: Email address and name for authentication
- **Progress Data**: Pin completion status, submissions, and achievements stored in Google Sheets
- **Local Cache**: Progress data cached locally on your device for offline access

## How We Use Your Information

- Authenticate users via Google Sign-In
- Display personalized progress for your explorer(s)
- Submit pin completions to your Circle's Pin Wizard
- Sync progress data across devices

## Data Storage

- User authentication handled by Google Sign-In
- Progress data stored in Google Sheets managed by your Adventure Guides Circle
- Local cache stored on your device using iOS UserDefaults

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is only accessible to:
- You (the parent/guardian)
- Your Circle's Pin Wizard (administrators)
- Other participants in your Adventure Guides Circle (as configured by your Circle)

## Children's Privacy

CompassPins is designed for families with children participating in Adventure Guides programs. We do not collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent through their Circle registration.

## Your Rights

- View your data through the app
- Request data deletion by contacting your Circle's Pin Wizard
- Sign out and clear local cache at any time
