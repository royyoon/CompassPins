## Description (4000 characters max)

CompassPins helps parents and children track their progress through the Adventure Guides Compass Journey program. Stay organized and motivated as your explorer earns pins across all four journey directions.

**Features:**

• **Track Progress**: See your child's pin achievements across all four journeys - North (Family), East (Nature), South (Community), and West (Fun)

• **Visual Journey Compass**: Beautiful interface showing progress in each direction with earned pins, rockers, and medallions

• **Pin Details**: View detailed requirements, examples, and visual guides for each pin activity

• **Submit for Approval**: Submit completed pins directly to your Circle's Pin Wizard for review

• **Multiple Children**: Support for families with multiple explorers - easily switch between children's profiles

• **Offline Support**: View cached progress even without internet connection

• **Adventure Names**: Personalized experience showing each explorer's unique adventure name

**For Adventure Guides Families:**

CompassPins is designed specifically for families participating in Adventure Guides programs. Parents can monitor their child's achievements, submit completed pins for approval, and help their explorers stay motivated on their compass journey.

**Requirements:**
- Google account registered with your Adventure Guides Circle
- Active participation in an Adventure Guides program
- Access provided by your Circle's Pin Wizard

Join your family's adventure today with CompassPins!

# Privacy Policy for CompassPins

**Last Updated:** October 27, 2024

## Information We Collect

CompassPins collects and uses the following information:

- **Google Account Information**: Email address and name for authentication
- **Progress Data**: Pin completion status, submissions, and achievements stored in Google Sheets
- **Local Cache**: Progress data cached locally on your device for offline access

## How We Use Your Information

- Authenticate users via Google Sign-In
- Display personalized progress for your explorer(s)
- Submit pin completions to your Circle's Pin Wizard
- Sync progress data across devices

## Data Storage

- User authentication handled by Google Sign-In
- Progress data stored in Google Sheets managed by your Adventure Guides Circle
- Local cache stored on your device using iOS UserDefaults

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is only accessible to:
- You (the parent/guardian)
- Your Circle's Pin Wizard (administrators)
- Other participants in your Adventure Guides Circle (as configured by your Circle)

## Children's Privacy

CompassPins is designed for families with children participating in Adventure Guides programs. We do not collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent through their Circle registration.

## Your Rights

- View your data through the app
- Request data deletion by contacting your Circle's Pin Wizard
- Sign out and clear local cache at any time
